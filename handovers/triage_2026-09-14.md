Overrule the record for a read-only reader, or keep it and have the mirror stay per-app?

  3. Which buckets: every bucket on prd RGW, including the dev/tst/uat-stage buckets, or prd-stage only?
  4. How long do overwritten and deleted objects stay in the dated archive folder before expiry (days)? The design left it as "a chosen number of days".
